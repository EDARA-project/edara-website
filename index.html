Educational Augmented Reality Authoring

Advancing Europe’s Digital Education Transformation with AI-Supported AR Learning

Project Overview

The project advances Europe’s digital education transformation by enabling educators to design AR learning supported by AI. EDARA strengthens digital readiness through evidence-based pedagogical innovation, enhances digital resilience through interoperable and inclusive authoring tools, and builds capacity by empowering educators across Europe and Ukraine to become confident creators of immersive learning using European open platforms.

EDARA will co-design and validate novel pedagogically-grounded AR authoring approaches together with educators. The project will develop and pilot immersive learning materials, evaluate their pedagogical impact, and deliver teacher training. Workshops, pilots and open training will support knowledge exchange and build a community of practice.

EDARA will deliver evidence-based methodological guidelines for educational AR authoring, improve 4 AR authoring platforms, produce and pilot materials, evaluate their pedagogical impact and publish them as OERs.

The EDARA project has received funding from the European Union’s Erasmus Plus programme, grant agreement 2026-1-NO01-KA220-HED-000462029.
